اللَّبنة
1 · الرحلة — Journey

Why: a first-time visitor needs to see the whole road before committing. Each of the 4 stations says what happens, how long it takes, what you need, plus one reassurance (voice note, private code, no bank tie-in). FAQ answers the four objections.

Trade-off: the longest page; the calculator is one tap away rather than on the page.

2 · الحاسبة أوّلاً — Tool-first

Why: useful before persuasive. Income + down payment → budget, monthly payment and what it buys; the result rides in the bottom bar and pre-fills the request form. Request tracking sits on the landing page too.

Trade-off: the numbers shown are a sample scenario and must stay labelled as such; the heaviest first screen to build.

3 · بالصور — Visual explainer

Why: diagrams do the explaining: a plot plan for ‘build on my land’, a bundle + contract for ‘land and house’, a building elevation, a rent-to-own timeline; brick vs block walls; a room that lights up what each finishing level adds.

Trade-off: needs real photos of Sfax cases to replace the placeholders; heaviest to keep up to date.

4 · الوضوح — Editorial

Why: trust comes from saying plainly what the platform does and does not do, with a few precise numbers and a lot of air. Fastest to load, easiest to translate to French.

Trade-off: the least visual; leans on copy quality and gives less guidance to someone who only skims.

5 · الرؤية — Vision

Why: positions اللَّبنة as national infrastructure: citizen, builders, craftsmen, owners and banks around one platform, Sfax delegation by delegation. Dark hero, glass cards, gold accents; the light block at the end keeps the paths readable.

Trade-off: more ‘brand’ than ‘help’; the per-delegation counts are illustrative and need real data behind them.
